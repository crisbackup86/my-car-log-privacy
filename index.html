Third-Party Services and Data Collection

The Application integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) and services to provide advertising, process in-app purchases, and maintain service functionality. These third parties may collect device identifiers and usage data directly from your device:

1. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions (RevenueCat & Google Play In-App Billing)

To process subscriptions or in-app purchases, the Application uses RevenueCat and Google Play Billing Services. RevenueCat collects app user IDs, transaction IDs, purchase history, device information, and IP addresses to manage digital entitlements and validate purchases.

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process unique identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, device hardware models, system crash data, and user interaction data with ads for ad serving, frequency capping, analytics, and fraud prevention.

  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management tool compliant with the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF), to collect your consent before serving personalized advertising or using non-essential tracking technologies. You can review or change your advertising consent choices at any time through this in-app consent form, accessible via the Application's settings.

This Application does not gather precise geolocation information about your mobile device.
